# Forged v7

## Roads with their own length
- Every goal is now a road you size yourself: 1-365 days. Use the stepper, type a number, or tap a preset (7 / 14 / 21 / 30 / 60 / 90).
- Each road starts the day you add it and runs on its own clock, so a 7-day coding road and a 30-day workout road can live side by side.
- Roads are listed shortest first. Each row shows its length, percent and streak.
- Goals created before v7 keep working: they are treated as 30-day roads on the shared cycle start. No data migration needed.

## Progress circles
- A small ring sits at the end of every road (end of the row on desktop, end of the day grid on phones).
  Green = done, red = missed, grey = still to go. It turns into a check mark when the road is forged.
- Per-road Restart (and Restart all). Restarting banks your stats first, so XP, awards and banners are never lost.

## Banner rewards
- Finishing a road unlocks a banner. The longer the road, the rarer the banner:
  1-3 UNLOCKED, 4-6 ELITE, 7-9 NO EXCUSES, 10-13 CONSISTENT, 14-20 FORGED,
  21-29 ASCEND, 30-44 BREAK LIMITS, 45-59 UNSTOPPABLE, 60-89 OVERCOME, 90+ LEGEND.
- While you work, the banner fills in with colour as your progress grows. The add form previews the banner a road will earn.
- Your Road page has a Banner rewards collection. Earned banners can be worn on your profile card.
- Files live in /banners.

## Scrollbars
- All scrollbars are custom slim pills (no browser default): they thicken on hover, turn into a gradient while dragging, and fade into the theme in light and dark mode.
- Smooth scrolling for the page and every scroll area, disabled automatically for people who prefer reduced motion.
- The page reserves scrollbar space so layouts no longer jump.

## Also
- XP bonus for forging a road scales with its length (200 XP at 30 days, same as before).
- Perfect Day now means every road that was running that day was checked.
- Toasts stack instead of overlapping.
- Report page shows roads forged and per-road totals (done/length).
- Cache-busting bumped to ?v=7.
